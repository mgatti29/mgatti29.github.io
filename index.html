About me

I am an observational cosmologist working across the full weak-lensing analysis chain, from the construction and calibration of survey data products to cosmological inference. This includes redshift calibration, shear catalogues, mass maps, and systematic-error tests.

I also develop higher-order statistics and simulation-based inference methods to extract information beyond standard two-point analyses, and I combine galaxy weak lensing with CMB lensing and the thermal Sunyaev–Zel’dovich effect.

I am based at the Institute of Space Sciences (ICE-CSIC) in Barcelona and am part of the ICE-CSIC Cosmology Group.

Research

Weak-lensing catalogues, mass maps, and redshift calibration

Understanding how weak-lensing measurements are built, calibrated, and validated, from galaxy redshifts and shear catalogues to mass-map reconstruction.

Weak-lensing inference beyond two-point statistics

Higher-order statistics, learned summaries, and simulation-based inference, with a focus on robust cosmological analyses.

CMB lensing, tSZ, and baryonic physics

Combining galaxy weak lensing with CMB secondary anisotropies to probe matter, gas, baryonic feedback, and cosmology across a broad range of scales and redshifts.
